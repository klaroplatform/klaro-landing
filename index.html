How we use research

Built on how students learn

Three findings from learning research, and where you'll see them in Klaro.

Learning and Instruction, 2006

Keep the source next to the explanation

A review of 50 studies found that people learn more when related information sits together, on the same page or at the same moment (Ginns, 2006).

In Klaro: each lecture moment opens at its book page.

Psychological Science, 2006

Testing beats rereading

Students who practised recalling a passage remembered more of it a week later than students who read it again (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006).

In Klaro: real past-paper questions on every topic.

Review of Educational Research, 2007

Feedback on how you did and what's next

Feedback helps most when it says where the student is going, how they are doing and what to do next (Hattie and Timperley, 2007).

In Klaro: marked papers, with feedback for each student.
