Your mark scheme. Your students' handwriting. Klaro marks to your scheme, and nothing goes out until you send it.

How it works

From scanned papers to sent results.

  1. 1

    Upload the papers and your scheme

    Add the question paper, your mark scheme, the total marks and each student's scanned paper as one PDF. Klaro matches each file to the student it's named after.

  2. 2

    Klaro marks every answer

    Klaro reads the handwriting and marks each question part against your mark scheme. You can leave the page once the uploads finish.

  3. 3

    Check, change and send

    Look over the marks and change any of them, with a reason. Send one result at a time, or every result that's ready with one button.

Your mark scheme

Marked against your scheme, question by question.

Klaro marks every question part against the mark scheme you uploaded with the exam, and checks that it adds up to the total marks you entered. You can open the marking scheme it works from at any time.
