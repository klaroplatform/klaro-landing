Minis for students

Your weak spots, one lecture moment at a time.

Minis picks the book units you miss most in practice and shows the moment of your tutor's lecture that teaches each one, then three quick checks.

Start learning

One idea, then three checks

Each mini is one unit of your course book, from your own tutor's course.

  • The moment it's explained

    A short clip of your tutor's lecture, at the moment it teaches one unit of your book. The book button opens that page.

  • Three checks from real past papers

    Each check is a past-paper question on the same unit, with its paper and year. One tap answers it, and you know straight away.

  • Cleared for today

    Three right and that unit is cleared. A unit you're still weak on stays on your list.

Sample course. Three moments of one mini, on a phone.

Powered by research. Rereading a passage came out ahead only on a test five minutes later. Two days and a week later, students who had practised recalling it remembered more. That's why every mini ends in checks.

Roediger, H. L., & Karpicke, J. D. (2006). Test-enhanced learning: Taking memory tests improves long-term retention. Psychological Science, 17(3), 249–255. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x

Weak spots first

Minis lists the units you missed most in past-paper practice, weakest first. Until you have weak spots, it starts from the beginning of the book.

About minis

Up to nine, weakest units first. Three right answers clear a unit for the day; a unit you're still weak on stays on your list.

A mini needs a clip of your tutor's lecture and three past-paper questions linked to the same unit, so how many a course has depends on how much of it is linked.
