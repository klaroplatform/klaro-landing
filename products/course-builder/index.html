What you upload

The material you already teach from, as files. You can add new papers and lectures later.

Your book or notes
PDF or Word, up to 100 MB a file. A book in several parts becomes one book.
Past papers and mark schemes
Drop each paper with its mark scheme and Klaro pairs them by name. Together they become your course's question bank.
Lecture videos
MP4, MOV or WebM, up to 1 GB each, in the order you teach them.
Klaro's question library
Past-paper questions for your subject, which you can switch on as well.

Linked for you, checked before it's published

The Klaro team links your material: each clip of your lecture to the part of your book it explains, and to the past-paper questions on it. Nothing reaches your students until it has been checked and published.

Powered by research. People learn more when an explanation and the material it explains are shown together, close in space and in time.

Ginns, P. (2006). Integrating information: A meta-analysis of the spatial contiguity and temporal contiguity effects. Learning and Instruction, 16(6), 511–525. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.learninstruc.2006.10.001
