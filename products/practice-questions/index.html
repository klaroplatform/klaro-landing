How it works

From your tutor's past papers to your screen.

  1. 1

    Your tutor adds the papers

    The past papers and mark schemes they already teach from, or Klaro's own library of past-paper questions for the subject.

  2. 2

    Klaro links them to the book

    The Klaro team files each question under your course book's chapters, and links it to the lecture clips that teach it.

  3. 3

    You answer and see where to look

    Multiple-choice answers are checked the moment you answer. Klaro shows the right answer, the book page and the lecture clip.
