Every question shows its paper

Type or say something like “10 questions on osmosis, some structured, a bit harder, nothing from 2019”.

Klaro plans the set from the papers, years and topics in your course, then picks each question from your question bank. It never writes new ones. Swap a question, drop one, or just ask: “add one on diffusion”.
