How it works

From your tutor's upload to your screen.

  1. 1

    Your tutor uploads

    The recorded lectures, the course book and the past papers they already teach from.

  2. 2

    Klaro connects them

    The Klaro team links each lecture clip to the part of the book it teaches and to its past-paper questions, then checks and publishes the course.

  3. 3

    You study side by side

    Watch with the book and the questions beside you. Go from a clip to its book page, or from the book to the lecture.
