How it works

From a join code to a clear next step.

  1. 1

    Join your tutor's course

    Enter the join code your tutor gives you. The course appears on your home page.

  2. 2

    Study and practise

    Watch your tutor's lectures and answer real past-paper questions. Klaro keeps your place in every lecture.

  3. 3

    See where you stand

    Each course shows the lectures you've finished, your quiz marks and what's next up.
