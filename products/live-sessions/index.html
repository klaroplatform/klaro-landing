Live sessions for tutors

Schedule a live class. Klaro sets up the rest.

Pick the course, the time and how long it runs, and Klaro sets up the Zoom meeting. Afterwards, the recording lands in your course, cut into clips linked to your book.

Start your course

Teach on Zoom, and the recording lands in your course

Your class sits on your teaching calendar, next to your quizzes and homework, and you start it on Zoom from there. Afterwards, the recording is cut into clips, each one linked to the part of your book you explained.

What your students see

From the calendar to the course

No links to send, and nothing to copy between apps.

  1. When you schedule it

    The meeting is made for you

    Klaro creates the Zoom meeting as you save. The class appears on your students' schedule and home page.

  2. 30 minutes before

    Your students are reminded

    Everyone in the course gets a reminder in Klaro that the class is about to start.

  3. At class time

    Start, join, present

    You press Start on Zoom. Your students press Join, and joining marks them present.

  4. Afterwards

    The recording, in clips

    The recording lands in your course, cut into clips linked to your book. You keep the last word on the register.
