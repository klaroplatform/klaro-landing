How it works

From a time on the schedule to a full class.

  1. 1

    Schedule a class

    Pick the course, the time and how long it runs. Klaro sets up the meeting when you save.

  2. 2

    Your students are told

    Everyone in the course gets an announcement, then a reminder 30 minutes before.

  3. 3

    Teach, and see who came

    Students press Join in Klaro and the class opens in its own tab. Joining marks them present.
