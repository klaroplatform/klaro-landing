faq

questions ( ・_・)ノ

where do i get a join code?

from your tutor. if your tutor isn't on klaro yet, send them this page.

who makes the lectures and questions?

your tutor sends their books and past papers. the klaro team prepares the lectures and questions and checks them before you see them.

is it in arabic?

yes. the whole app works in arabic and english, and flips right to left in arabic.

do i need to download anything?

no. klaro runs in your browser, on your phone or laptop.
