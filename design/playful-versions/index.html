what we took from heyclicky

  • everything in lowercase, casual and short
  • text faces like (^ ω ^) instead of emoji
  • little mac windows as frames
  • "it's early!" note from the team
  • a wall of notes, simple prices, faq

what stays klaro

  • the purple and gold, the K and its spark
  • Manrope, with a serif for one word or two
  • real product pieces: lecture, book page, past-paper question, join code
  • a line of arabic in every hero

before any of this goes live

  • the student quotes are examples; swap in real ones from the beta
  • the 450 egp price is an example
  • arabic has no lowercase: the arabic page gets the same casual voice in egyptian arabic instead
