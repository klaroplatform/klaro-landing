faq

you asked ( ・_・)ノ

how do i join a course?

make an account, then type the join code your tutor gives you. the course appears on your home page.

who makes the lectures and questions?

your tutor sends their books and past papers. the klaro team prepares the lectures and questions and checks them before you see them.

is klaro an ai chatbot?

no. this page talks like a chat for fun. klaro is a study app: lectures, the book and practice questions, prepared and checked by people.

i don't have a code. now what?

browse the market and pick a course, or ask your tutor to bring their course to klaro.
