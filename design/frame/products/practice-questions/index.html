Osmosis 2024 · Paper 1

Four pieces of potato of equal mass are each placed in a different solution for 30 minutes. In which solution does the potato gain mass?

ADistilled water
B0.5 mol/dm³ sucrose solution
C1.0 mol/dm³ sucrose solution
D2.0 mol/dm³ sucrose solution
Correct Explained on p. 48
Book · p. 48

Water molecules move freely through a partially permeable membrane. Osmosis is the net movement of water from a dilute solution to a more concentrated one through such a membrane.
