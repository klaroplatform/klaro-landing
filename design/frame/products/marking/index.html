Mark scheme · Question 2(c)Chemistry 0620

Describe how to make pure, dry crystals of copper(II) sulfate from copper(II) oxide and dilute sulfuric acid. [4]

  • Add excess copper(II) oxide to warm dilute sulfuric acid
  • Filter to remove the unreacted copper(II) oxide
  • Heat the filtrate to the point of crystallisation
  • Leave to cool and crystallise, then dry the crystals on filter paper
