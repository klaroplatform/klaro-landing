Keep the source next to the explanation

Students learn more when the words and the picture they explain sit together, not apart (Ginns, 2006).

In Klaro: the lecture opens at its book page.

Psychological Science, 2006

Testing beats rereading

Answering practice questions helped students remember a week later better than reading again (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006).

In Klaro: real past-paper questions after each lecture.

Review of Educational Research, 2007

Feedback on how you did and what's next

Feedback works when it says where the student is going, how they're doing and what to do next (Hattie and Timperley, 2007).
